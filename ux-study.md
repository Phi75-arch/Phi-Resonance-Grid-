UX & Human Factors Evaluation Plan

This document defines the experimental methodology used to evaluate Φ-Resonance as a human-centered developer tool.


---

Study A: Emotional Response Comparison

Objective

Compare emotional and cognitive responses between Φ-Resonance and a conventional linter.

Method

Same codebase analyzed by both tools

Participants rate (1–5):

Feeling judged vs supported

Clarity of feedback

Stress or frustration

Desire to refactor



Hypothesis

Non-coercive framing produces lower resistance and higher engagement.


---

Study B: Voluntary Action Rate

Objective

Measure whether developers act more when not forced.

Method

Developers run Φ-Resonance on personal projects

No requirement to change code


Metrics

Percentage of suggestions acted upon

Time-to-action

Types of suggestions accepted or ignored



---

Study C: Cognitive Load Assessment

Tool

Simplified NASA-TLX

Metrics

Mental demand

Frustration

Sense of control


Comparison

Traditional linter session vs Φ-Resonance session


---

Qualitative Feedback

Participants are asked:

How did this tool make you feel?

Did any suggestion feel insightful?

Did any feel irrelevant or distracting?


Open-ended responses are considered first-class data.
